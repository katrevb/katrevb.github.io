About Kathleen

I’m Kathleen Joie T. Belda, a student from Cebu Technological University – Argao Campus, born and raised in Dalaguete, Cebu. I wouldn’t call myself a creative person—I don’t paint, I don’t sketch, and I definitely can’t compose music. But for some reason, I’ve always been drawn to art. Photography, film, and design. I always catch myself wondering: How do they do it? What makes something truly captivating?

That curiosity has always lingered. Instead of just admiring from a distance, I decided to try. No natural talent, no grand artistic vision—just the urge to understand.But I tend to quit things easily. Sometimes, I walk away and convince myself it was never mine to begin with. And yet, something always pulls me back—a quiet whisper, a feeling I can’t quite name. Maybe I’ll never be an artist. Maybe my hands will never shape something lasting. But at least, I tried. At least, I let curiosity lead me where it wanted to go.

I don’t know where this journey will take me. Maybe I’ll never master one thing. But I do know that I’ll always be curious, always fascinated by the process. And for now, maybe that’s enough.
